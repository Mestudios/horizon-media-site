Refunds

Last updated November 16, 2026

We don't take payments

Horizon Media shows our team's work and passes your booking request to one of our photographers. We don't charge you anything, and we never receive or hold your money.

Prices and payment

Each photographer sets their own prices. When you book, the photographer who takes the job agrees the price and how to pay directly with you, and you pay them, not Horizon Media.

Cancellations and refunds

Because you pay the photographer, any refund, cancellation or new date is between you and that photographer, on the terms you agreed when you booked. Please ask about their cancellation and refund terms before you pay.

If something goes wrong, message us on Instagram or email horzion.media418@gmail.com. We'll help you reach the photographer and try to sort it out, but we can't give refunds ourselves because we never received the money.

Free things

Photo requests made through this site and our free presets cost nothing, so there is nothing to refund.

This page doesn't take away any rights you have under the law. For the rules on booking, see the terms.
