Cookies

Last updated November 17, 2026

This site uses as little as it can. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and nothing here follows you to other websites. Everything listed below is either needed for the site to work or something you choose to turn on, so the site doesn't need to ask before using it. The one thing from another company, Google's booking form, only loads when you ask for it.

Things from other companies

  • Google Forms on the booking page. It loads only after you press "Load the booking form". Google may then set its own cookies, covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • Instagram links open Instagram in a new page. Nothing loads from Instagram while you browse this site.
  • Fonts and every photo are served from this site itself, so your visit isn't shared with a font or image service.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser's settings. The site works the same without hm_visitor. To forget your light or dark choice, use the switch again or clear this site's data in your browser.
