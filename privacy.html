Privacy

Last updated November 17, 2026

This page explains what the Horizon Media website collects when you visit, what happens when you book with us, and how to ask for a photo to be taken down.

What this site collects

The site has no visitor accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. Two things happen when you open it:

  • One cookie. The homepage, portfolio and car pages set a cookie called hm_visitor. It holds a random number that lasts 30 minutes. We use it only to count how many people are on the site at once, so we can send smaller photos when the site is busy. It does not identify you and is not shared with anyone.
  • Server records. Our hosting company, Netlify, receives your IP address and browser type in order to deliver the page, as every web host does. To keep the site safe, an address that loads pages hundreds of times in a minute, or that types wrong passwords on our team's sign-in page, is written to our hosting log and can be blocked for up to an hour. The counts and the block list behind this are kept under a scrambled form of the address and are used for nothing else.

You can block or delete the cookie in your browser settings. The site works the same without it. Everything the site stores is listed below.

If you use the light/dark switch, your choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. It is never sent to us.

Members of the Horizon Media team also have a sign-in page for adding photos. Signing in sets a second cookie, hm_studio, which keeps them signed in for 12 hours, or for 30 days if they choose to stay signed in on their own device. Visitors never get it.

Things from other companies

  • Google Forms on the booking page. It loads only after you press "Load the booking form". Google may then set its own cookies, covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • Instagram links open Instagram in a new page. Nothing loads from Instagram while you browse this site.
  • Fonts and every photo are served from this site itself, so your visit isn't shared with a font or image service.

To forget your light or dark choice, use the switch again or clear this site's data in your browser.

Booking with us

Our booking page can show a Google Form inside it. The form only loads when you press "Load the booking form"; then Google receives your IP address and browser details, as any site you visit does, and may set its own cookies.

The form asks for your name, how to reach you, and the details of your game or shoot, such as the sport, place and time. Optional questions ask how you heard about us and whether you have any questions or concerns. The form may also ask you to agree to our terms and to say whether anyone we will photograph is under 18. It does not collect your email address unless you type it in, and it has no file uploads.

What you type is stored by Google and sent to us. We use it only to reply to you and arrange the coverage or car shoot you asked about, and we pass it only to the Horizon Media photographer who takes your booking. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else. Google's handling of that information is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Asking for a photo

The portfolio and car pages have a "Request this photo" button. If you use it, we receive the photo you asked about, your name, the Instagram name or email you give, and any message you write. It is stored with our hosting company, Netlify, and only the Horizon Media team can see it. We use it only to reply to you about that photo, and we delete it once your request is handled. We keep a count of how many times each photo has been asked for, with no names or contact details attached.

To stop spam, the site counts how many requests come from one internet address in an hour. That count is kept only briefly and is not stored with your request.

Instagram

Links on this site open Instagram. Once you are there, Instagram's privacy policy applies. This site does not load anything from Instagram while you browse it.

Our photos

Every photo on this site was taken by a member of the Horizon Media team, and each one names its photographer.

Our sports photos are taken at games and school events, with media credentials wherever the event issues them. Many of the athletes in them are under 18. An athlete, or their parent or guardian, can have a photo taken off this site at any time: see asking for a photo to be removed.

Our car photos are taken at car meets and car shoots. We blur license plates in the car photos on this site, and where we know who owns a car, we credit them.

Asking for a photo to be removed

If you are in a photo on this site, you are the parent or guardian of someone who is, or a car you own is shown, you can ask us to remove it from this website. You don't need to give a reason.

  1. On the portfolio or car page, open the photo and press "Request removal" under it. It starts an email or an Instagram message to us that names the photo for you.
  2. Or contact us yourself: message us on Instagram at @hor1zonmedia, or email horzion.media418@gmail.com, and tell us which photo it is (the page it is on and its title, or a screenshot).
  3. We take it off this site and let you know when it's gone.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information through the booking form or a photo request, message us on Instagram or email horzion.media418@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you from a booking or photo request, or ask us to delete it, by messaging us on Instagram or emailing horzion.media418@gmail.com.

Changes

If what the site collects changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
