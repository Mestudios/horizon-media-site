Privacy

Last updated November 13, 2026

This page explains what the Horizon Media website collects when you visit, what happens when you book with us, and how to ask for a photo to be taken down.

What this site collects

The site has no visitor accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. Two things happen when you open it:

  • One cookie. The homepage, portfolio and car pages set a cookie called hm_visitor. It holds a random number that lasts 30 minutes. We use it only to count how many people are on the site at once, so we can send smaller photos when the site is busy. It does not identify you and is not shared with anyone.
  • Server records. Our hosting company, Netlify, receives your IP address and browser type in order to deliver the page, as every web host does. If one address requests the page hundreds of times in a minute, that address is written to our log so it can be blocked.

You can block or delete the cookie in your browser settings. The site works the same without it.

If you use the light/dark switch, your choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. It is never sent to us.

Members of the Horizon Media team also have a sign-in page for adding photos. Signing in sets a second cookie, hm_studio, which keeps them signed in for 12 hours. Visitors never get it.

Booking with us

The booking button opens a Google Form. What you type there, such as your name, contact details and event or car details, is stored by Google and sent to us. We use it only to reply to you and arrange the coverage or car shoot you asked about. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else. Google's handling of that information is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Asking for a photo

The portfolio and car pages have a "Request this photo" button. If you use it, we receive the photo you asked about, your name, the Instagram name or email you give, and any message you write. It is stored with our hosting company, Netlify, and only the Horizon Media team can see it. We use it only to reply to you about that photo, and we delete it once your request is handled.

To stop spam, the site counts how many requests come from one internet address in an hour. That count is kept only briefly and is not stored with your request.

Instagram

Links on this site open Instagram. Once you are there, Instagram's privacy policy applies. This site does not load anything from Instagram while you browse it.

Our photos

Every photo on this site was taken by a member of the Horizon Media team, and each one names its photographer.

Our sports photos are taken at games and school events, with media credentials wherever the event issues them.

Our car photos are taken at car meets and car shoots. We blur license plates in the car photos on this site, and where we know who owns a car, we credit them.

Asking for a photo to be removed

If you are in a photo on this site, you are the parent or guardian of someone who is, or a car you own is shown, you can ask us to remove it from this website. You don't need to give a reason.

  1. Message us on Instagram at @hor1zonmedia, or email horzion.media418@gmail.com.
  2. Tell us which photo it is: the page it is on and its title, or a screenshot of it.
  3. We take it off this site and let you know when it's gone.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information through the booking form or a photo request, message us on Instagram or email horzion.media418@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you from a booking or photo request, or ask us to delete it, by messaging us on Instagram or emailing horzion.media418@gmail.com.

Changes

If what the site collects changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

For the rules on using the site and its photos, see the terms.
