Privacy

Last updated November 10, 2026

This page explains what the Horizon Media website collects when you visit, what happens when you book coverage, and how to ask for a photo to be taken down.

What this site collects

The site has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. Two things happen when you open it:

  • One cookie. The homepage sets a cookie called hm_visitor. It holds a random number that lasts 30 minutes. We use it only to count how many people are on the site at once, so we can send smaller photos when the site is busy. It does not identify you and is not shared with anyone.
  • Server records. Our hosting company, Netlify, receives your IP address and browser type in order to deliver the page, as every web host does. If one address requests the page hundreds of times in a minute, that address is written to our log so it can be blocked.

You can block or delete the cookie in your browser settings. The site works the same without it.

Booking coverage

The booking button opens a Google Form. What you type there, such as your name, contact details and event information, is stored by Google and sent to us. We use it only to reply to you and arrange the coverage you asked about. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else. Google's handling of that information is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Instagram

Links on this site open Instagram. Once you are there, Instagram's privacy policy applies. This site does not load anything from Instagram while you browse it.

Our photos

Our photographers take photos at games and events with media credentials issued for those events.

Asking for a photo to be removed

If you are in a photo on this site, or you are the parent or guardian of someone who is, you can ask us to remove it from this website. You don't need to give a reason.

  1. Message us on Instagram at @hor1zonmedia.
  2. Tell us which photo it is: the page it is on and its title, or a screenshot of it.
  3. We take it off this site and let you know when it's gone.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information through the booking form, message us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you from a booking, or ask us to delete it, by messaging us on Instagram.

Changes

If what the site collects changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

For the rules on using the site and its photos, see the terms.
