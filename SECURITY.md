# Security

Thank you for helping keep the Horizon Media website and the people in our
photos safe. Many of them are under 18, so we take reports seriously.

## Reporting a problem

Please report security problems privately. **Don't open a public issue or
pull request**, and don't post details anywhere public until we've fixed it.

- Email **horzion.media418@gmail.com** with "Security" in the subject line.
- Tell us what you found, where (the page or address), and the steps to see
  it happen. Screenshots help.
- If you can, tell us how to reach you for follow-up questions.

We'll reply to confirm we got your report, and let you know when it's fixed.
We're a small team, so please give us reasonable time before sharing
anything publicly.

## What's in scope

- The website at horizonmedia-sports.netlify.app.
- The code in this repository.

Not in scope: Netlify, Google Forms, Instagram or other services we use.
Please report problems with those to the company that runs them.

## Please don't

- Access, change, download or delete anyone else's information, including
  photo requests, removal requests or the team's studio. If you come across
  personal information by accident, stop, don't keep a copy, and tell us.
- Try to sign in to the team's studio with guessed or stolen passwords.
- Overload the site, send spam through its forms, or run automated scans that
  flood it with traffic.
- Use social engineering on our team or the people in our photos.

## Good-faith research

If you follow this page, act in good faith and give us a chance to fix the
problem, we won't take action against you for your report. We don't offer
payment for reports.

## Not a security problem?

To ask for a photo of you, your child or your car to be taken down, see the
[privacy page](https://horizonmedia-sports.netlify.app/privacy.html#removal).
For anything else, email us or message
[@hor1zonmedia on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/hor1zonmedia/).
